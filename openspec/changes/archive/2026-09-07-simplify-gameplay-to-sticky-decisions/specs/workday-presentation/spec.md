## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The workstation presents a passive laptop and expanded sticky workspace
The workstation SHALL keep the illustrated laptop visible as a passive prop, remove task, application, AI-review, and quick-action controls, and distribute readable inline stickies through the freed space without covering the status rail.

#### Scenario: A supported viewport renders gameplay
- **WHEN** the workstation is viewed at desktop or minimum touch size
- **THEN** the passive laptop, status rail, clock, sticky paging/backlog, and lifecycle controls remain visible and usable
